Foundations — Beginner Course

Short lessons to get you trading confidently. Practical, non-technical, and focused on real outcomes.

What you will learn

  • How currency pairs work (base vs quote)
  • What pips and pipettes are, and how to calculate profit/loss
  • Spreads, commissions and execution basics
  • Reading price charts and placing your first order
  • Simple risk rules to protect your capital

Lesson 1 — Currency pairs & pips (summary)

Currency markets trade pairs like EUR/USD. The first currency is the base, the second is the quote. If EUR/USD = 1.1200, one euro buys 1.12 US dollars. A pip is the smallest standard price movement (usually the 4th decimal for most pairs). Understanding pips helps you calculate trade size and risk.

Lesson 2 — Spreads, execution & order types

The spread is the difference between the bid and ask price — it is a small, built-in cost of trading. Market orders execute instantly at available prices, while limit orders wait for a desired price. Learn the difference and how slippage can affect small accounts.
