Technical Indicators: Practical Use

A concise, trader-focused guide to indicators and how to combine them into a reliable edge.

Core indicators we use

  • EMAs (20 / 50 / 200) — trend filtering and dynamic support/resistance.
  • RSI (14) — momentum and divergence signals; avoid extreme entries alone.
  • MACD — trend strength and cross confirmation.
  • ATR — volatility sizing and stop placement.

Example strategy (educational)

Only take trades in the direction of the 50 EMA. Use pullbacks toward the 20 EMA together with an RSI reading above 40 (for longs). Place a stop at 1 × ATR below entry and target a 1.5–2× reward-to-risk.

Practical tips

  1. Keep indicators simple — less is more.
  2. Test on a demo account before risking real capital.
  3. Keep a trade journal: entry reason, stop, target, and outcome.
