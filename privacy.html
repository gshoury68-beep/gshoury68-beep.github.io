Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TradeLearn collects, uses, discloses and protects your personal data when you use our website and Services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR where applicable.

2. Data we collect

  • Booking and contact details: when you book a session via Calendly or a contact form, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number and booking details.
  • Communications: messages you send to us via email, chat or contact forms.
  • Usage data: anonymized analytics data collected by our analytics provider (Plausible) where enabled and only after cookie consent.

3. How we use your data

We use personal data to provide and manage bookings, deliver course materials, communicate with you about sessions and to respond to support requests. We may also use aggregated, anonymized data for service improvement and analytics.

4. Third-party services

We use third-party services to operate our booking and communication functions. These may include (but are not limited to):

  • Calendly: used for scheduling and booking. Calendly is an independent data controller and its own privacy policy applies. Bookings made through Calendly may be processed by Calendly and its subprocessors.
  • Tawk.to (optional): if integrated for live chat, Tawk.to may collect message content and contact details. Tawk.to is configured only after you provide a property ID.
  • Plausible Analytics: a privacy-first analytics provider. We only enable Plausible after the visitor accepts cookies. Plausible collects minimal, non-identifying metrics.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to improve the website experience and to enable analytics. Our cookie banner requests consent for analytics cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing local storage for this site or using your browser settings.

6. Data retention and your rights

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the Services and to comply with legal obligations. Where applicable, you may exercise rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to make a data request, contact us at: support@support.com.
