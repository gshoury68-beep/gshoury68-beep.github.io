About TradeLearn

Practical, instructor-led trading education for Forex and Crypto — designed for beginners and professionals who want repeatable skills, not hype.

Who we are

TradeLearn is a team of experienced traders, risk managers and educators dedicated to delivering clear, hands-on training that focuses on reproducible processes: reading price action, managing risk, designing strategies and keeping disciplined execution routines.

Our mission

To empower traders worldwide with the practical skills needed to protect capital and pursue consistent performance through education, practice and mentorship.

What we teach

  • Market structure and price-action fundamentals
  • Risk management, position sizing and trade psychology
  • Strategy design, backtesting and journaling
  • How to use indicators as complementary tools — not trading signals

Why choose us

  • Practical lessons with live demonstrations and clear routines
  • Beginner tracks and advanced workshops for ongoing development
  • GDPR-aware booking and privacy-respecting analytics

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide trading signals?

No. All content is strictly educational. We teach methodology, execution and risk management — we do not provide buy/sell signals or personalised financial advice.

Is this service GDPR compliant?

We respect EU data protection laws. Bookings are handled via Calendly (a third-party service) and analytics are privacy-first (Plausible) and only enabled after cookie consent.

How do I get support?

Use the contact form or the chat widget (when configured) to reach our support team. For data requests, see our Privacy Policy for the contact details and process.
